FAQ

How do I switch between English and Japanese?

In the menu bar, choose Podtrim > 言語 / Language, then English or 日本語. The app reopens in that language. New episodes find fillers and transcribe in that language. Episodes you already made keep the language they were made in.

The first transcription is slow

The first time only, Podtrim downloads the transcription model (about 0.9 GB). This takes a few minutes. After that, a 40-minute episode takes about 2 minutes.

The first filler search is slow

The first time you find fillers in an English episode, Podtrim downloads the English filler model (about 0.95 GB). After that, it runs on your Mac without downloading.

Where do I get an AI key (API key)?

In the Show Notes panel, open AI Settings and choose an AI. A link to the page where you create that provider's key appears. Gemini (Google) has a free tier with usage limits; Google uses what you send on the free tier to improve its products, and people may read it, so Google asks you not to send personal information. Claude (Anthropic) and OpenAI are paid. Paste the key and click Save. Podtrim shows what it sends and where, so click Agree and Save if that's fine. Nothing is sent until you agree. The key is stored encrypted on your Mac and never shown on screen. Without AI, you can still make the transcript and a prompt to paste into any chat app.

The microphone doesn't work

Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone on your Mac and turn on Podtrim.

I can't find my recordings in Finder

Recordings made in Podtrim are kept inside the app. On the recording screen, use Export… on that row to save it to any folder in Finder.

The tracks don't line up at the start

In the top bar, click Adjust, then Realign… under Alignment. Drag each row left or right to match, then click Use This Position. Next time, clap once together when you start recording, and the tracks line up automatically.

I deleted an episode by mistake

Episodes deleted with Delete Episodes… go to the Trash on your Mac. In the Trash in Finder, right-click the episode and choose Put Back. The original recordings and exported files are not deleted.

I want a refund

Apple handles refunds for apps bought on the App Store. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I want to report a bug

Use Help > Send Feedback… in the menu, or the feedback form. Tell us what you did and what happened, and paste what you copy with Help > Copy Diagnostics. That helps us fix it faster.
