Your first episode

Here's the flow from adding recordings to one file for your podcast host and a draft of the show notes. It's the same for a solo show (1 track) or an episode with guests (up to 8).

1. Get the recordings ready

  • One recording per speaker (WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A, FLAC, and more. From video, such as MP4 or MOV, only the audio is used). Per-speaker recordings from remote recording services work as they are.
  • You can also record in Podtrim. Switch the Edit | Record control at the left of the top bar to Record, then click ● Record. The first time, your Mac asks for microphone access. Click Allow.
  • Clap once together when you start recording, and Podtrim uses that sound to line up everyone's tracks automatically. It works without the clap, too.

2. Make a new episode

  1. Click + New Episode (⌘N), select all the recordings, and click Open. You can also drag them onto the window.
  2. Check the episode name and click Create. Podtrim tightens the pauses where nobody is talking, evens out the levels, marks crosstalk, and opens the episode (about a minute for a 40-minute episode). Noise, backchannels, and fillers are handled in the background after it opens.

The original recordings are never changed. All edits are kept separately with the episode.

3. Listen and fix

  • Click Review (C) in the top bar to hear each trimmed gap and crosstalk spot one by one, from just before it. If it sounds right, press → for the next one.
  • If too much was cut, double-click the red line to restore it. To remove a part, select it and press ⌘⌫ (delete for everyone and close the gap).
  • Made a mistake? Press ⌘Z. Saving is automatic. For all keys, choose Help > Keyboard Shortcuts in the menu.

4. Export

  1. Click Export at the top right. The first time, click Choose Folder… to pick where to export (remembered on this Mac).
  2. Check the name, the format (WAV or MP3), and the loudness (−16 LUFS is recommended), then click Export.

5. Draft the show notes

  1. When the export finishes, the Show Notes panel opens below. Click Create, and Podtrim transcribes the exported audio on your Mac. The first time, it downloads the transcription model (about 0.9 GB), which takes a few minutes.
  2. To use AI, open AI Settings at the top right of the panel, choose an AI, paste that provider's key (API key), and click Save. Podtrim shows what it sends and where, so click Agree and Save if that's fine. The AI writes title ideas, a description, and chapters.
  3. Without AI, copy the prompt and paste it into a chat app such as Claude or ChatGPT to get the same result.

6. Upload to your podcast host

Click Show in Finder in the Show Notes panel, drag the exported file into your podcast host, and paste the title and show notes.
