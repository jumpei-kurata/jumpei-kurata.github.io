Podtrim

A podcast editor for Mac

Start from one recording per speaker (1 to 8). Podtrim tightens the pauses, cleans up noise, marks fillers and crosstalk, and gets you to one file for your podcast host and a draft of the show notes. Recording and editing all happen on your Mac.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 13 or later.
