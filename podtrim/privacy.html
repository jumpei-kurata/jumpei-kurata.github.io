Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This page explains how Podtrim, a podcast editing app for Mac, handles information. Recording, transcription and editing happen on your Mac. Podtrim sends nothing to the developer's servers.

Recordings, transcripts and edits

The recordings you add or record in Podtrim, the transcripts, your edits and the files you export stay on your Mac. Transcription also runs on your Mac. None of this is sent to the developer.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record on the recording screen. You can turn off access at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

AI for show notes (only if you choose it)

AI is off by default. Only if you choose an AI provider (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI) in the AI settings, enter your own API key, and agree to what will be sent, Podtrim sends the following directly from your Mac to that provider when you click “Create”. When you click “Save”, or click “Create” before agreeing, Podtrim first shows a window with what will be sent and to whom:

  • the transcript text (which may include the words and names of the people you talked with)
  • the show name, the episode name, your title preferences, today’s date and the next episode number

The audio itself is never sent. If the provider is busy, Podtrim may send the request again to the same provider. Requests are made with your own key under your agreement with that provider. The developer does not receive this content. The provider handles what you send under your agreement with it and its own terms. The developer never passes your information to any other company. For how each provider handles it, see their terms:

  • Google (Gemini): https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. Content sent on the free tier is used by Google to improve its products and may be read by people. Google asks users not to submit personal information to the free tier. If that concerns you, use a paid tier, another provider, or turn AI off.
  • Anthropic (Claude): https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms. Anthropic states that it does not, by default, use content sent through its API to train its models (per its terms; this may change).
  • OpenAI: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data. OpenAI states that it does not, by default, use content sent through its API to train its models (per its terms; this may change).

If other people took part in an episode, please let them know before sending it to an AI. If you do not agree, choose “Off” in AI Settings, and Podtrim still creates the transcript and a prompt you can copy, on your Mac. You can withdraw your consent at any time by choosing “Off” in AI Settings and clicking “Save”, or by deleting your key.

Your API key is stored encrypted on your Mac and is never sent to the developer.

Other network access

  • Transcription model: the first time you transcribe, Podtrim downloads the model (about 0.9 GB) once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its content delivery network). No recordings or text are sent.
  • English filler model: the first time you search for fillers in an English episode, Podtrim downloads the model (about 0.95 GB) once from GitHub (github.com and its content delivery network). No recordings or text are sent.
  • Show RSS feed: only if you enter an RSS URL in the show settings, Podtrim reads that feed to count the next episode number. Feeds whose URL starts with http are read without encryption.
  • The app’s screen and its internal service talk to each other only inside your Mac (127.0.0.1). This does not connect to the internet or other devices.

What is stored on your Mac, and how to delete it

  • Episodes (cuts, waveforms, transcripts, show-note drafts and so on): “Delete Episodes…” moves them to the Trash.
  • Settings and API keys: the key can be deleted with “Delete Selected AI's Key” in AI Settings. This also withdraws your consent for that provider.
  • The transcription model, the English filler model and the app log are kept in the app’s own storage.

After you delete Podtrim, its data container (in your home folder → Library → Containers) may remain. To remove everything, move it to the Trash as well.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Podtrim does not use analytics tools, advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Purchases

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple (App Store). The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account information. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Information the developer receives

The developer never receives your name, email address, recordings or transcripts, with two exceptions:

  • What you send through the feedback form (your message, the app and macOS versions, any diagnostic information you paste, and a reply address if you give one). The form is a Google Form. It is used only to reply to you and to improve the app. To have it deleted, ask through the form.
  • Crash reports from Macs whose settings allow sharing with app developers (delivered by Apple). They are used only to fix problems.

Children

The developer does not collect information from anyone, including children. When you use AI, what is sent goes directly from your Mac to the provider you chose, as described above.

Changes

When this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.
