Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This explains what information ReplayClip, a delayed-replay camera for practice (dance, golf, the gym and more), handles and how. ReplayClip does not collect your name, email address, video or sound, and does not send them anywhere. To improve the app, it collects only anonymous usage counts.

Information we collect

To improve the app, it sends anonymous usage counts (for example: the review screen was opened, a save finished, Compare was used, the names of the buttons and drawing tools used, the purchase screen was shown, the result of a purchase) to the analytics service TelemetryDeck (Germany). Along with these, it sends general information such as how often and how long the app is used, the device model, the iOS and app versions, language and region, and time zone. The identifier that tells devices apart is turned into a form that cannot be reversed on your device before it is sent.

Video, photos, the lines you draw, sound, location, your name and your email address are never sent. The counts are not combined with other information and are not used for advertising or tracking. There is no account to create.

Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used to play your video and sound back on the screen with a delay of a few seconds to a few minutes.

Only when you turn on "Keep me in frame" in Settings (it is off by default), the app finds people in the recorded video on the review screen and zooms the view toward them. This is done only on your device. The results are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

Recorded video and sound are kept in a temporary area on your device that only the app uses. The oldest parts are deleted automatically, and anything left over is deleted the next time you open the app. Video and sound never leave your device.

You can withdraw camera and microphone access at any time in the iPhone or iPad Settings app.

Motion sensor (tilt)

The app uses your device's tilt to decide the screen orientation on iPhone and to show the "Level line" from Settings. It is used only on your device and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Photo library

The app saves a video clip to Photos only when you tap "Save". It saves a marked-up still (one picture of the paused frame with the lines and angles you drew) to Photos only when you tap "Save marked-up still to Photos" on the review screen. It only asks for permission to add to your photo library, and does not read your library. In Compare, only the videos you choose yourself are read, on your device only.

Settings

Settings such as the delay length are stored only on your device. They are not sent off your device and are removed when you delete the app.

On-device server

To play video, the app uses a server that runs only inside your device (a local server on 127.0.0.1). It does not connect to the internet or to other devices.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

For usage analytics, the app uses only TelemetryDeck, as described in "Information we collect" above. It does not use advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases (ReplayClip Pro)

(From version 1.1. Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases.)

Monthly, yearly and lifetime purchases, restoring purchases and redeeming codes are processed by Apple (the App Store). The app and the developer never receive your payment details (such as card numbers) or your Apple Account information.

Whether you can use the app is checked only from the App Store purchase records on your device. The app does not send any information to the developer's servers for this check.

The app reads a settings file that decides which plans the purchase screen shows from the developer's website (jumpei-kurata.github.io). It only reads the file and does not send any information (the website host, GitHub, handles connection records just as it does when you open any web page).

Cancellations and refunds are handled by Apple. You can cancel the monthly or yearly plan in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Children

The app collects only anonymous usage counts and does not collect information that identifies children.

What the developer receives

The developer does not receive your name, your email address, your video, or your sound. The developer only sees the anonymous usage counts described in "Information we collect" above.

The only exception is what you choose to send through the feedback form below. It goes through Google Forms, and only what you write reaches the developer. It is used only to answer you and to improve the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will be revised.
