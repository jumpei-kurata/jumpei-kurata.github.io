Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This page explains what information ShadowMate, an app for shadowing practice with your own audio and video files, handles and how. ShadowMate does not collect or send your name, email address, audio, or transcripts. To improve the app, it collects anonymous usage counts. For people who use it for free, it also shows ads.

What we collect (usage counts)

To improve the app, ShadowMate sends anonymous usage counts to the analytics service TelemetryDeck (Germany). Examples: a file was opened, a transcription was made, an A-B loop was set, the speaking pause was used, a practice comparison was made, the names of buttons that were used, the purchase screen was shown, the result of a purchase, and a short reason when something failed. It also sends general information such as how many times and how long the app was used, the device model, the OS and app versions, language and region, and time of day. The identifier for your device is turned into a value that cannot be reversed on the device before it is sent.

We never send audio, video, transcripts, recordings of your voice, file names, your name, or your email address. We do not combine these counts with other information or use them for tracking. There is no account to sign up for.

Your audio and video files

Files you open are kept in a private place for this app on your device and are used only for playback and practice. They are never sent off your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Transcription

Transcription happens only on your device (iOS speech recognition on iPhone, Whisper running on the device on Android). Neither your audio nor the transcribed text leaves your device.

On Android, the first time you transcribe, the app downloads the transcription data (a trained model) once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Only the usual connection information for a download is sent; your audio is never sent.

Microphone (practice recordings)

The app records your voice only when you tap to repeat in practice. Recordings stay on your device and are used only to compare with the original and to check which words you said (with on-device transcription). They are never sent off your device. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your device's Settings.

Advertising

For people who use the app for free, ShadowMate shows ads using Google AdMob. To deliver ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect information such as:

・Your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers
・Your IP address and the approximate region it indicates
・Which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
・How the app is used, such as when it is opened, and performance information such as crash logs and launch time

Google handles this information under its own responsibility, not on the developer's behalf. Under the data protection terms between Google and the developer, Google must comply with the data protection laws that apply to it. For how Google handles this information, see Google's page.
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On iPhone, the app may ask for permission to track your activity across apps so that ads can be tailored to you. The app works the same if you decline. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In some regions, such as Europe, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent about ads when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the app's Settings.

People on the paid plan do not see ads.

Purchases (Premium)

Monthly and yearly purchases and restoring purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Neither the app nor the developer receives your payment information (such as card details) or account information. Whether Premium is active is checked only with the purchase records on your device.

Cancellations and refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone, you can cancel a subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Settings and practice records

Your playback position, A-B loops, speed, transcription results, settings, and practice streak are stored only on your device. They are never sent off your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app only collects anonymous usage counts and does not collect any information that identifies a child.

What the developer receives

The developer never receives your name, email address, audio, or transcripts. The developer only sees the anonymous usage counts described above.

The exception is what you send through Settings → Send feedback in the app or the form below. It goes to Google Forms, and only what you write (plus the app version, device model, and language that the app fills in for you) reaches the developer. It is used only to reply and to improve the app.

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
